Our History

Why Purple?

Purple Coffee was born from a moment of hope during one of life’s most uncertain seasons. During the pandemic, when the world felt especially difficult, my faith became a source of strength and comfort. One day, while listening to a message by Dante Gebel, I was deeply moved by the meaning of the color purple a symbol of dignity, purpose, transformation, and hope. In that moment, a dream took root in my heart: one day, my coffee brand would carry that name. Purple Coffee honors that beginning while welcoming every person, regardless of their story, background, or beliefs. It is a place where everyone can feel valued, find connection, and enjoy a thoughtfully crafted cup of coffee. Because purple is more than a color. It is a reminder that every life has value, every story matters, and even difficult seasons can give birth to something beautiful.

Mision

At Purple Coffee, we create meaningful experiences through specialty coffee. Every beverage is crafted with excellence, passion, and attention to detail, while creating a welcoming space where people feel valued and connected. More than serving coffee, we seek to share hope, love, and purpose in every cup.

Vision

To become a specialty coffee brand recognized for its quality, excellence, and purpose. We aspire to build a community that inspires connection, hope, and transformation, growing from New York into new cities while remaining true to our essence: serving with love, honoring every story, and reminding people that their lives have value and purpose.
