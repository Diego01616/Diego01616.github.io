Our History

Why Purple?

Born six years ago, Purple Coffee was inspired by a color with a story of faith, identity, and rarity.

01

Faith

Royalty Purpose Sacrifice

Purple carries a deep biblical symbolism, associated with royalty, dignity, and sacrifice.

02

Identity

Voice Dignity Belonging

Purple became a symbol of identity and self-expression in African-American culture, beautifully reflected in The Color Purple.

03

Legacy

Rarity Craft Value

Ancient purple dye was made from mollusks, making it one of the rarest and most valuable colors in history.
